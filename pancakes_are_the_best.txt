FADE IN:

INT. KITCHEN - MORNING

SARAH (30s), hair disheveled, stares at an empty fridge. Her stomach GROWLS loudly.

SARAH
(to herself)
Perfect. Another gourmet breakfast of... air.

She opens the pantry. Nearly bare except for a dusty box of pancake mix.

SARAH (CONT'D)
(sighs)
Guess it's pancakes or starvation.

MONTAGE - PANCAKE MAKING DISASTER

- Sarah reads the box instructions, squinting
- She dumps mix into a bowl, creating a flour cloud
- Milk splashes everywhere as she measures
- The first pancake burns black while she's distracted by her phone

SARAH (CONT'D)
(flipping the charcoal disk)
Martha Stewart, eat your heart out.

Her ROOMMATE MIKE (20s) enters, sniffing the air suspiciously.

MIKE
Is something on fire, or are you cooking?

SARAH
Very funny. Want some pancakes?

MIKE
(examining the burnt pancake)
Are they supposed to look like hockey pucks?

Sarah flips a second pancake - this one golden brown and perfect.

SARAH
(proudly)
See? I'm getting the hang of this.

MIKE
(takes a bite)
Holy... these are incredible!

SARAH
(surprised)
Really?

MIKE
Sarah, you need to enter the town pancake competition. The winner gets five thousand dollars.

SARAH
(laughing)
Right, because my two successful pancakes qualify me for—

MIKE
(interrupting, mouth full)
These aren't just pancakes. They're... they're magic.

Sarah takes a bite. Her eyes widen.

SARAH
Oh my god. These ARE incredible.

FADE TO:

EXT. TOWN SQUARE - COMPETITION DAY

Banner reads: "ANNUAL FLAPJACK CHAMPIONSHIP." Sarah stands at a cooking station, nervous but determined.

ANNOUNCER (V.O.)
Ladies and gentlemen, the final round!

Sarah flips her pancakes with newfound confidence. The crowd watches in awe as her pancakes seem to float through the air.

ANNOUNCER (V.O.)
And the winner... Sarah Martinez!

Sarah holds up her trophy, pancakes stacked high on her plate.

SARAH
(to the crowd)
Sometimes the best things come from the worst mornings!

The crowd cheers. Mike runs up and hugs her.

MIKE
I told you they were magic!

SARAH
(grinning)
Pancakes really are the best.

FADE OUT.

THE END
